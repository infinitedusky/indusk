cockpit-redraw

PlanBriefResearch

The cockpit's nav, plan page and brief page are redrawn to look as Sandy meant them — the nav from the original cockpit mockup, with every plan, Scorecards and a width you can drag — and each look is approved once. Re-approving a changed brief becomes something only a person can do.

Expectations

  1. Each cockpit look is approved about once per plan, not redrawn and re-approved in rounds.
    • Measure: approval entries per look name per plan, from approved.json, against brief-as-the-contract's three rounds for the plan page
    • Look: after the next three plans that change the admin's looks
  2. Plan names in the nav are read whole.
    • Measure: whether Sandy widens the nav and keeps it wider, read from the remembered width
    • Look: two weeks after landing

Promises

This plan makes

  1. the-nav-widens-by-dragging (state). Above phone width a person can drag the nav's edge to make it wider or narrower within limits, and the admin remembers that width.
  2. only-a-person-reapproves (state). Re-approving a changed brief is done only by a person: indusk plans reapprove run from an agent's tool call is refused, as approving a look already is.

Looks

This plan makes

  1. brief.writing. The brief page while the plan is written.
  2. brief.approved. The brief page once the plan is approved.
  3. nav.wide. The nav dragged wider, every plan name whole.
