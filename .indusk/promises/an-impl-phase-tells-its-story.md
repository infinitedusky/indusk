Every phase of a new impl opens with a plain paragraph saying what it does for the whole plan, and every task is one plain sentence with its technical detail beneath it, written for someone who has worked on the project but knows none of its shorthand: no row labels, no abbreviations, nothing named by a nickname or an unclear pronoun; an impl that leaves out the paragraph or the sentence, or puts a row label in either, is refused.
