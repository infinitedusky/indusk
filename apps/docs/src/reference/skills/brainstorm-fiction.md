# Brainstorm: fiction

`/brainstorm-fiction` takes a fiction idea from a seed to a piece line that
passes a threshold, before any outline exists. It converges: loose
brainstorming diverges and stops when the ideas run out; this one ends when
the strongest remaining objection is one the piece is about. Its output is
one thing, the piece line, recorded as level zero of the outline file that
[`/write`](/reference/skills/write) then drills down.

It is the first of a family. Each genre gets its own brainstorm skill with
the whole process and its own axes; opinion posts will ask for a claim,
evidence, and an objection where fiction asks for a want, a cost, and a
turn. The shared skeleton is extracted when the second one arrives.

## The process

1. **The seed.** What the user wants to do, in their words, before anything
   is judged: a subject, the question it raises, and the form.
2. **Ten books.** Near the seed or pointing at it. The list is the context.
3. **What each gives.** One thing per book: what this piece wants from it,
   an axis it proves can be broken, or what this piece refuses. A book that
   gives nothing comes off the list; every thing given becomes a question
   in the round.
4. **Purpose.** Why the project exists, separate from whether the product
   succeeds; one line each. A collaborator named in the purpose gets a
   running list of questions only they can answer.
5. **The register.** The spine in a handful of verbs, and every idea filed
   against it: in, out or demoted, or weather. Weather is in the piece's
   world, over the protagonist's head and not the reader's, and owes no
   payoff. Density in the room, economy on the spine.
6. **The round.** The adversarial pass, each question armed by a book,
   every objection labelled **open**, **answered**, or **declared**.
7. **The axes check.** One want; a countable cost; a turn; an earned
   verdict; a form the reader can hold. A five-row table, yes or no each.
8. **The threshold.** Conforms on every axis but one, the one named. "No
   objection left" is the wrong threshold.
9. **The claims list.** Every fact the piece leans on, with confidence,
   verified before prose.

The outline file carries a ledger at the top, decided, open, and weather,
restated in chat at every checkpoint so the user never has to read the file
to know the state.

## Invocation

- `/brainstorm-fiction <plan>` loads or creates the plan's outline and
  begins at the seed.
- Without the slash: the description names stories, novellas, novels, and
  screenplays that do not yet have an outline, so "I have an idea for a
  novel" routes here. Once a piece line exists, `/write`.

## Where it came from

The generation-ship novella (now in the `writing-workbench`, under
`.indusk/planning/generation-ship-story/`) was the first run, and the process was written down as it happened: the
labels came from an objection that read as a verdict, the register from a
kitchen-sink moment, the axes check from a threshold that was declared
passed without being checked.
