# InDusk — promises in your editor

Every promise at the line that keeps it, its health from your project's
telemetry, and one click from a break to your own Claude working on the fix.
It reads `indusk promises health --json` and writes nothing to your project.

Install it with `indusk editor install`. See the guide:
https://infinitedusky.github.io/dusk/guide/promises-in-your-editor
