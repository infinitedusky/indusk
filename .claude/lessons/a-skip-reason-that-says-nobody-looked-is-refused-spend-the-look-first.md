# Do not skip falsify or cleanup with a reason that says nobody looked ("no hypothesis formed", "nothing to decompose") — the audit rejects it, and spending the look is what finds the real defect

In finished-rituals-stay-done both rituals were first skipped with thin reasons. The audit rejected both. Doing the cleanup look found the same "is falsification finished" check written in both the retrospective gate and the admin, which became Build Phase 3 (one finishedRitualsOf in the package). Rewriting the falsification reason to claim a probe, without a probe that matched, was also called out as overstating what rows A6–A9 pinned.

What to do: run the ritual, or skip with a reason that names what was actually read and found. A reason that cannot be checked against a row or file is a claim, not evidence.
